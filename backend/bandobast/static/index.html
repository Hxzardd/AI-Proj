Commander override. VETO forbids that unit→zone order; the solver re-optimises the rest and re-tasks the unit elsewhere, so ETAs — and even which zones get served — can shift. PIN locks an order the commander insists on; the solver keeps it fixed and optimises around it. Because units are scarce and interdependent, vetoing different units changes the plan by different amounts: remove the only ambulance that can reach a medical zone and the solver must find a costlier replacement (objective drops more); veto a redundant unit and the objective barely moves.
